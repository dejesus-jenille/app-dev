# Stranger Things

## Genre  
**Science Fiction**, *Horror*, Mystery, Drama

## Created by  
The Duffer Brothers

---

## Synopsis  

> “Friends don’t lie.”  

Stranger Things is set in the 1980s in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana. It follows a group of kids who uncover supernatural mysteries involving a secret government lab and an alternate dimension called the Upside Down.

---
